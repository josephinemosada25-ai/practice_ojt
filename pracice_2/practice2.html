WATTY

I hate how people call it weakness when someone walks away.

It takes courage to admit when something isn't working anymore. especially after trying so hard

Leaving behind a life someone once built or a future they hoped for , knowing it might hurt anymore than staying

It's never easy.

Someime's bravery isn't about holding on tight.

It's about letting go when you have to.
